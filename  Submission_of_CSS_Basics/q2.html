Welcome to Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan's former capital, famous for its wooden temples, classical gardens, and traditional wooden houses. It is a city where centuries-old culture blends peacefully with modern life.

Ancient Temples

Fushimi Inari Shrine, with its thousands of vermillion torii gates, is one of the most photographed spots in all of Japan.

Local Cuisine

From delicate kaiseki dinners to street-side matcha sweets, Kyoto's food scene reflects centuries of refined tradition.

Cherry Blossoms & Autumn Leaves

Kyoto transforms twice a year — pink cherry blossoms in spring and fiery red maple leaves in autumn draw visitors worldwide.
